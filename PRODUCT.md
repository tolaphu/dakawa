# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static-exportable React site built with the Sites starter. The delivered experience must also remain understandable as a polished HTML demo and be portable to a custom WordPress theme later.

## Users

- Households in Hội An and Đà Nẵng that need water delivered quickly and want clear information about sizes, bottle exchange, service area, and contact channels.
- Offices, hotels, homestays, restaurants, events, and other organizations that need recurring supply or a quotation.
- Prospective retailers or distributors evaluating a DAKAWA partnership.

## Product Purpose

Present DAKAWA as a trustworthy local producer of purified bottled and large-format water, help customers choose the right format, and convert them into a structured order, quotation request, or distributor inquiry.

## Positioning

DAKAWA is presented as a Hội An producer that combines direct local delivery with visible production and quality proof, rather than as a generic multi-brand water reseller.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from mobile search, Facebook, Zalo, WhatsApp, referrals, and hospitality networks. The public contact details supplied in the project are 33B Lý Thái Tổ, Hội An, 0985 959 790, 0235 652 0123, and admin@dakawa.vn. Publicly referenced formats are 20L, 7L, 1.5L, 500ml, and 330ml.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Responsive marketing and conversion site with product browsing, quick-order interaction, B2B quotation form, distributor registration, delivery-area information, quality/process content, FAQ, and contact actions.
- Vietnamese-first demo with language-switching affordance for Vietnamese, English, and a third-language demo. The final third language remains to be confirmed before production.
- Product prices, certifications, production claims, customer logos, delivery promises, and testimonials must not be invented. Demo-only product imagery and values must be labeled as illustrative.
- Future implementation target is a custom WordPress theme with manageable content types and multilingual content.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: DAKAWA.
- Use the supplied circular D water logo as the visual authority.
- Preserve the logo's deep ocean blue and vivid red as the primary brand colors.
- Do not use Gotek branding or its visual system.
- The interface must feel authored and production-minded, not like a generic AI landing-page template.
- Use a real icon library instead of emoji, Unicode symbols, or hand-drawn interface icons.
- The frontend is intentionally adapted from the Acuas drinking-water template by HTML Codex/ThemeWagon. Preserve its HTML Codex attribution unless DAKAWA purchases the credit-removal license.

## Product Principles

- Make ordering water obvious on mobile without hiding the producer story.
- Place proof beside claims and clearly mark unverified demo content.
- Give households, businesses, and distributors separate conversion paths.
- Use real DAKAWA assets when available and high-quality illustrative assets only where clearly labeled.
- Keep the future WordPress content model simple enough for the client's staff to manage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Primary actions must remain readable and reachable on small screens, touch targets must be at least 44px, forms need real labels and recovery guidance, motion must respect reduced-motion preferences, and contrast must support older and low-vision users.
